What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. It can be useful when writing assignments, articles, essays, descriptions, posts and other types of content with word limits.

AIHub's Word Counter lets you enter or paste text and quickly see the number of words in your content.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Open the Word Counter tool.
  2. Type or paste your text into the text area.
  3. The tool analyzes your text.
  4. Check the displayed word count.
  5. Edit your text and check the count again whenever needed.

Why Use AIHub's Word Counter?

  • Count words quickly.

  • Helpful when working with word limits.
  • Check text directly in your browser.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • No software installation required.

When is a Word Counter Useful?

A word counter can be useful for school assignments, application writing, blog posts, articles, social media content and other writing tasks where the length of the text matters.

Checking the word count before submitting or publishing content can help you stay within the required limit.
