What is a Quiz Generator?

A Quiz Generator helps students, teachers and creators create questions for learning and revision. Quizzes can make study sessions more interactive and can help learners check their understanding of a topic.

AIHub's Quiz Generator can create questions with answer options and an answer key based on the topic and settings selected in the tool.

How to Use the Quiz Generator

  1. Enter the topic you want to create a quiz about.
  2. Select the number of questions.
  3. Choose the difficulty level when available.
  4. Generate the quiz.
  5. Answer the questions and check the answer key.

Why Use AIHub's Quiz Generator?

  • Create quiz questions quickly.
  • Useful for revision and practice.
  • Generate multiple-choice questions.
  • Includes an answer key for checking.
  • Suitable for different educational topics.
  • Works directly in your browser.

Tips for Better Quiz Practice

Choose a specific topic when creating a quiz. After completing the questions, review the incorrect answers and revisit the related topic in your textbook or study material.

For better revision, try quizzes with different difficulty levels and avoid checking the answer key until you have completed all the questions.
